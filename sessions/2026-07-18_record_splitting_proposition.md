# Session log — 2026-07-18 — record-splitting proposition

## Intent

Sixth pull on the braided-clock thread: formalize the section 14 remark —
syndrome records cannot serve as logical clock readout — as a proved
proposition with a quantitative cost lemma.

## What was written

`bridges/clock_syndrome_record_splitting.md`:

- **Definitions.** Logically transparent instrument (Kraus operators
  commuting with the logical algebra on the code sector); logical clock as
  a one-parameter family generated by a nontrivial logical operator.
- **Proposition 1.** Transparency implies record distributions exactly
  independent of the clock phase, for single instruments and for adaptive
  record-conditioned sequences. Two-line proof via commutation and
  cyclicity; the induction handles feedback because the conditioning
  variables are themselves phase-independent.
- **Proposition 2.** Contrapositive: clock-reading records are logically
  central.
- **Lemma 3.** For the weak binary probe of a logical observable at
  strength kappa: record Fisher information
  \(J=\kappa^2a'^2/(1-\kappa^2a^2)\), exact conjugate-coherence multiplier
  \(\sqrt{1-\kappa^2}\) per probe, and the budget
  \(C_N\le e^{-J_{\mathrm{tot}}/2}\) in the gentle limit — formalizing the
  empirical \(e^{-S^2/2}\) tick-cost observation from Experiments A/D.
- **Corollary 4.** Record splitting: an encoded braided clock needs a
  transparent syndrome channel plus a central clock channel with
  \(J_{\mathrm{clock}}\le-2\ln F_{\mathrm{floor}}\). Degenerate case: on
  the trivial code the only transparent instruments are uninformative,
  which is why single-qubit Experiment A had a narrow interval.
- **Honesty section.** Related known results (Eastin-Knill-type
  obstructions, covariant-QEC no-gos, Faist et al., Woods-Alhambra) are
  cited as adjacent and in some directions stronger; no priority claimed
  for the commutation argument. Local content: the explicit
  Fisher/coherence budget in simulation-native form and the two-channel
  persistence reading.
- **Numerical anchors.** Experiment E: `0.9967` retention under 72
  transparent measurements vs `0.0008` for the same-strength central
  probe, against predicted \((0.64)^{18}\approx3.3\times10^{-4}\);
  Experiment B's budget optimum as the empirical solution of the Lemma 3
  tradeoff.
