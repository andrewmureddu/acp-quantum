## Main Formal Result

The note proves a conditional tensor-product theorem.

Assume:

1. each subsystem already has a local complex Hilbert branch space;
2. independent composition is represented by a bilinear map
   \(\beta:\mathcal H_A\times\mathcal H_B\to\mathcal H_{AB}\);
3. independent branch weights multiply:
   \(\|\beta(v,w)\|^2=\|v\|^2\|w\|^2\);
4. the composite space is generated by superpositions of such products.

Then the universal property of the tensor product gives a unique linear map

$$
T:\mathcal H_A\otimes\mathcal H_B\to\mathcal H_{AB}
$$

with

$$
T(v\otimes w)=\beta(v,w).
$$

Norm multiplicativity plus polarization shows \(T\) is an isometry, and the
span-generation axiom makes it onto. Therefore \(T\) is unitary and

$$
\mathcal H_{AB}\cong \mathcal H_A\otimes\mathcal H_B .
$$

## Main Interpretation

This does two useful things.

First, it shows why direct sum is the wrong law for independent coexistence:

- \(\mathcal H_A\oplus\mathcal H_B\) encodes "A or B";
- independent preparation requires "A and B together";
- tensor product is the unique norm-compatible realization of that joint
  preparation under the stated axioms.

Second, it reframes entanglement cleanly:

- product states are the images of independent preparations;
- entangled states are generic superpositions inside the tensor-product space;
- entanglement is therefore not a separate composition rule but a consequence
  of the same superposition structure already present locally.
