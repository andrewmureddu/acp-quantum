## Structure of the essay

1. **Prologue** — zero proper time along null paths; light cannot keep a
   diary; "light is the ink, not the diary."
2. **Born of Loss** — every photon is a receipt for another system's
   surrendered distinction; persistence requires exporting distinctions, and
   light is the export channel.
3. **The Journey That Takes No Time** — light is not a persistent system in
   the ACP sense (no operational tempo, no productive interval of its own);
   it is a two-ended relational observable, incorruptible in transit because
   it has no interior.
4. **The Honest Courier** — light as the physical carrier of \(R_\partial\);
   Zurek redundancy / quantum Darwinism; structural bias toward broadcasting
   pointer observables while failing to deliver coherences to any fragment —
   the Knill-Laflamme-shaped selectivity (syndrome, not logical state) built
   into the universe's default record channel.
5. **Darkness Is Also a Sentence** — Fermat/stationary phase as a selection
   rule over competing predictions (mirror room); dark fringes and null
   records as informative constraints, \(I(G;R_0)>0\); absorption lines and
   shadow astronomy.
6. **The Long Letters** — finite \(c\) turns distance into history; the CMB
   as the oldest record in delivery; anisotropies as noise-as-signal at
   cosmological scale; redshift as degradation that loses intensity but keeps
   structure.
7. **Where the Messenger Runs in Place** — lensing as geometry's watermark;
   horizons defined by light; singularity inadmissibility stated as the
   severing of the record channel; Hawking radiation as conjectured delayed
   delivery. Proven/derived/conjectural boundaries of the derivation program
   preserved explicitly in the prose.
8. **The Speed Limit Is a Gift** — finite \(c\) as the universe's
   verification latency \(\tau_v\); instant verification would crystallize
   the present away; restraint-power reading of the maximal courier's ceiling
   as everyone else's coordinate system.
9. **Death as Delivery** — photons end only by absorption; light's whole
   existence is a completed act of communication; closing line: "Light does
   not persist. Light is what persistence is written in."
