## Structure of the essay

1. **Prologue** — darkness is not a substance (no particle, no carrier, no
   speed); pairing with light's essay: light is a relation with no duration,
   darkness a relation with no carrier; darkness appears on both pages of the
   persistence contract.
2. **Born of Veto** — two dark births: obstruction (shadows, transit
   photometry) and cancellation (dark fringes as negotiated verdicts requiring
   coherence); darkness as structured record, not background.
3. **Faster Than Light, Carrying Nothing** — superluminal shadow edges break
   no law because darkness transmits nothing; resolution of the apparent
   tension with informative null records: constraint vs. causation; null
   records are relational information legible only against a prior; every
   silent detector performs a hypothesis test. Cites the project's mirror-room
   result (dark records strictly sharpen posterior inference).
4. **The Oldest Measurement** — Olbers' paradox: the dark night sky as the
   naked-eye evidence against an infinite eternal static cosmos; finite age +
   redshift resolution; Poe's 1848 anticipation; darkness waiting millennia
   for a prior capable of reading it.
5. **Read From What Is Withheld** — Fraunhofer absorption lines as
   composition-by-theft; dark matter as the structured mismatch between
   luminous and dynamical ledgers (honestly framed: inferred from positive
   gravitational records, its darkness being refusal of the photon channel);
   dark energy discovered in a supernova dimming — a deficit of light; ~95%
   of the cosmic budget known only as constraint. Gestures at OP-17's lensing
   null-record upgrade.
6. **The Portrait** — the 2019 EHT image of M87* as a photograph whose
   subject is a darkness; horizon darkness as legislated by causal structure,
   pivoting darkness from testimony to protection.
7. **The Sanctuary** — quantum-optics dark states / coherent population
   trapping; the project's DFS encoding as a dark state of collective
   dephasing, with \(I(\mathrm{logical};\mathrm{environment})\approx 0\) as
   the protection mechanism itself; Zeno effect and measurement-induced
   transitions: unwatched intervals as where entanglement grows; the
   black-hole interior's darkness read as the protection clause executing,
   with late decodable return via horizon radiation explicitly marked as the
   project's conjecture.
8. **The Floor Under the Dark** — perfect darkness does not exist: vacuum
   fluctuations, spontaneous emission as vacuum-stimulated, Casimir effect;
   ACP reading: an exactly-zero channel would be crystallized, so
   \(H_{\mathrm{floor}}>0\) has a tabletop ancestor in the un-switch-offable
   vacuum.
9. **Chiaroscuro (epilogue)** — all-light universe = Zeno freeze /
   crystallization by total publication; all-dark universe = dissolution by
   no coordination; the productive interval is painted in both tones. Closing
   line paired with light's: "Darkness does not conceal. Darkness is what
   persistence keeps."
