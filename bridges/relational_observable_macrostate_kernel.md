## 1. Purpose

The quantum-gravity derivation program needs one load-bearing object:

$$
P_{\ell,\Delta}(m'|m).
$$

This note constructs the first ACP-native version of that object. The target is
not yet a full theory of quantum gravity. It is a finite-resolution relational
kernel that can carry the three diagnostics the program needs:

$$
H_{\ell,\Delta}(m),
\qquad
I(G_\ell;R_{\partial}),
\qquad
I(L_R;R_{\partial}^{\mathrm{early}}\mid G_\ell).
$$

Here \(G_\ell\) is the coarse geometry sector, \(R_{\partial}\) is the boundary
record, and \(L_R\) denotes protected interior logical/microstate information.
The kernel is the bridge from the finite toy collapse model to a domain-native
gravitational object.

## 2. Honesty Boundary

**Formal in this note.** Given a finite family of relational observables, a
finite macrocell partition, and either a classical admissible flow or a quantum
channel/history rule, the induced kernel is a normalized stochastic kernel on
the retained admissible support. If the observables are relational, the kernel
does not depend on coordinate representatives.

**Derived from ACP.** If a collapse kernel approaches zero conditional future
entropy or loses normalization to inadmissible singular support, the description
has left the productive interval. A mechanism-changing completion is then
required before the coordination floor is breached.

**Conjectural in physics.** The microscopic completion that supplies the
required channel for spacetime is quantum gravity, with the correct algebra of
relational observables, boundary records, protected interior information, and
classical limit.

**Still open.** This note does not derive the physical Hilbert space of gravity,
the exact gravitational path integral, or Einstein dynamics from ACP. It defines
the kernel that those future derivations must instantiate.

## 3. Relational Observable Algebra

Let \(R\) be a compact gravitational region with boundary or exterior access
\(\partial R\). A coordinate field value \(g_{\mu\nu}(x)\) is not an admissible
macrostate label, because \(x\) is gauge-dependent. The admissible labels must
be relational.

Choose a finite operational reference structure

$$
\mathcal F_\ell
=\{X^0,\ldots,X^3;\partial R;\ell\},
$$

where the \(X^\mu\) may be matter clocks/rods, asymptotic clocks, dust fields,
boundary cuts, horizon slices, or another finite reference frame supplied by
the candidate theory. The role of \(\mathcal F_\ell\) is not to pick preferred
coordinates. Its role is to define which finite distinctions an observer can
actually record at scale \(\ell\).

Let \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{rel},\ell}(R)\) be the finite relational observable
algebra generated by:

1. boundary charges and coarse horizon/exterior data,

$$
M,\ J,\ Q,\ A_{\mathrm{hor}},\ \Phi_{\partial,k};
$$

2. relationally smeared geometric and matter observables,

$$
\mathcal O_i[f_i;X]
=
\int_R d^3y\,\sqrt q\,
f_i(X(y))\,\mathcal O_i(q,K,\phi,\pi_\phi;y);
$$

3. null and positive boundary-record bins,

$$
R_{\partial}
=
(R_+,R_0,Y_{\partial}),
$$

including detections, non-detections, radiation modes, shadow/ringdown bins,
and other finite exterior records.

The observables must either commute with the relevant constraints or be defined
after adjoining the reference-frame degrees of freedom and imposing the
constraints. In both cases, two diffeomorphism-related representatives define
the same macrocell.

## 4. Macrocells

Choose a finite observable vector

$$
O_\ell
=
(O_1,\ldots,O_n)
\subset
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{rel},\ell}(R),
$$

with finite bin maps

$$
b_i:\mathrm{spec}(O_i)\to \{1,\ldots,N_i\}.
$$

A relational macrocell is the joint bin label

$$
m
=
(b_1(O_1),\ldots,b_n(O_n))
$$

restricted to states or histories satisfying the gravitational constraints and
the admissibility conditions:

$$
\mathcal M_\ell
=
\{m:\ C_m\cap \mathcal S_{\mathrm{adm}}\neq\varnothing\}.
$$

For the collapse problem it is useful to split the label schematically as

$$
m=(G_\ell,B_\ell,L_\ell),
$$

where:

- \(G_\ell\) is the geometry sector visible at the chosen resolution, such as
  compactness, expansion, coarse curvature, trapped-surface indicators, or
  horizon-sector labels;
- \(B_\ell\) is the boundary/exterior record sector;
- \(L_\ell\) is the residual interior logical sector that should not be exposed
  by early boundary records except through admissible coarse geometry labels.

This split is not an assertion that gravity literally factorizes into three
tensor factors. It is a finite diagnostic partition. In algebraic language,
there may be a shared center generated by charges, constraints, or area-like
operators. The privacy condition should be read relative to the chosen center.

## 5. Quantum Kernel

Let \(\{E_m\}_{m\in\mathcal M_\ell}\) be the finite POVM or projective
coarse-graining associated with the macrocells:

$$
E_m\geq 0,
\qquad
\sum_{m\in\mathcal M_\ell}E_m=I_{\mathrm{adm}}.
$$

Let \(\mathcal E_\Delta\) be the candidate theory's finite-time physical
channel on admissible states, or the effective channel induced by a constrained
history rule over the verification interval \(\Delta\). For a representative
state \(\rho_m\) supported in macrocell \(m\), define

$$
P_{\ell,\Delta}(m'|m)
=
\mathrm{Tr}
\left(
E_{m'}\,\mathcal E_\Delta(\rho_m)
\right).
$$

If the theory is stated in decoherent-histories language, the same object is
written schematically as

$$
P_{\ell,\Delta}(m'|m)
=
\frac{
\mathrm{Tr}\!\left(C_{m\to m'}\,\rho\,C_{m\to m'}^\dagger\right)
}{
\sum_{\tilde m\in\mathcal M_\ell}
\mathrm{Tr}\!\left(C_{m\to \tilde m}\,\rho\,C_{m\to \tilde m}^\dagger\right)
},
$$

where \(C_{m\to m'}\) is the class operator for histories that begin in the
relational cell \(m\) and end in \(m'\). The denominator is not cosmetic: if it
vanishes or if probability mass has escaped into inadmissible singular support,
the description has failed as a physical channel.

**Proposition 1 (finite relational kernel).** If \(\mathcal E_\Delta\) is CPTP
on the admissible support and \(\{E_m\}\) is a finite relational POVM resolving
that support, then \(P_{\ell,\Delta}(m'|m)\) is a normalized stochastic kernel
on \(\mathcal M_\ell\). If the effects \(E_m\) are relational observables, the
kernel is invariant under diffeomorphism changes of representative data.

**Proof.** Positivity follows from \(E_{m'}\geq0\) and complete positivity of
\(\mathcal E_\Delta\). Normalization follows from
\(\sum_{m'}E_{m'}=I_{\mathrm{adm}}\) and trace preservation:

$$
\sum_{m'}P_{\ell,\Delta}(m'|m)
=
\mathrm{Tr}(\mathcal E_\Delta(\rho_m))
=
\mathrm{Tr}(\rho_m)
=1.
$$

Relational invariance follows because each \(E_m\) is defined on equivalence
classes or Dirac/reference-frame observables, so replacing a representative by
a diffeomorphic representative leaves all bin labels and traces unchanged.
\(\square\)

## 6. Classical Pushforward Kernel

Classical GR supplies a useful failure baseline. Let
\(\mathcal S_{\mathrm{cl}}\) be a constraint-satisfying classical initial-data
space with admissible subset \(\mathcal S_{\mathrm{adm}}\), coarse map
\(\sigma_\ell:\mathcal S_{\mathrm{adm}}\to\mathcal M_\ell\), and classical
flow \(\Phi_\Delta\) where it exists. For a finite measure \(\mu_m\) on the
cell \(C_m=\sigma_\ell^{-1}(m)\), define the unconditioned admissible
pushforward mass

$$
K_{\ell,\Delta}^{\mathrm{cl}}(m'|m)
=
\mu_m
\left(
\{s\in C_m:
\Phi_t(s)\in\mathcal S_{\mathrm{adm}}\ \forall t\leq\Delta,
\ \sigma_\ell(\Phi_\Delta(s))=m'\}
\right).
$$

The total retained mass is

$$
Z_{\ell,\Delta}^{\mathrm{adm}}(m)
=
\sum_{m'}K_{\ell,\Delta}^{\mathrm{cl}}(m'|m).
$$

Classical GR gives a normalized kernel on admissible macrocells only when

$$
Z_{\ell,\Delta}^{\mathrm{adm}}(m)=1.
$$

If \(Z_{\ell,\Delta}^{\mathrm{adm}}(m)<1\), probability mass has flowed into
geodesically incomplete, divergent, or otherwise inadmissible support. One can
renormalize the surviving mass,

$$
\tilde P_{\ell,\Delta}^{\mathrm{cl}}(m'|m)
=
\frac{
K_{\ell,\Delta}^{\mathrm{cl}}(m'|m)
}{
Z_{\ell,\Delta}^{\mathrm{adm}}(m)
},
$$

but this is no longer the original physical channel. It is a postselected
description that has discarded the missing coordination.

**Proposition 2 (classical collapse failure, kernel form).** Let \(m\) be a
finite relational collapse macrocell. Suppose that, under the classical
focusing assumptions, a positive \(\mu_m\)-measure subset of \(C_m\) reaches an
inadmissible singular set before \(\Delta\), and no new admissible variables or
boundary transfer are supplied. Then the classical pushforward either:

1. fails normalization on admissible macrocells,

$$
Z_{\ell,\Delta}^{\mathrm{adm}}(m)<1;
$$

2. or, after hard exclusion/postselection, defines a different theory whose
surviving kernel has discarded the singular branch without a decodable boundary
record;
3. or, in the limiting case where all surviving admissible continuations
concentrate on one macrocell, violates the ACP future-entropy floor,

$$
H_{\ell,\Delta}(m)\to 0.
$$

Thus classical collapse cannot be the complete admissible kernel for a
nontrivial persistent gravitational region.

**Proof sketch.** By assumption, a positive-measure subset of the initial cell
has no image in \(\mathcal S_{\mathrm{adm}}\) at time \(\Delta\). Its mass is
therefore absent from the sum over admissible macrocells, so the retained mass
is less than one unless new admissible support is added. If the missing support
is simply deleted and the remainder renormalized, the resulting kernel is
conditioned on non-singular survival and contains no record of the deleted
coordination. This is an added selection rule, not classical evolution. If
focusing also concentrates the retained futures into a single cell, the
conditional entropy of the retained channel tends to zero, which is the
crystallization boundary. \(\square\)

This proposition is deliberately modest. It does not prove a microscopic
quantum-gravity mechanism. It proves that the classical pushforward is the
wrong kind of object once collapse drives positive mass outside the admissible
state space.

## 7. Diagnostics

The kernel supplies the ACP quantum-gravity diagnostics.

**Future entropy.**

$$
H_{\ell,\Delta}(m)
=
-
\sum_{m'}P_{\ell,\Delta}(m'|m)
\log P_{\ell,\Delta}(m'|m).
$$

The cosmic coordination floor requires

$$
H_{\ell,\Delta}(m)
\geq
H_{\mathrm{floor}}(m;\ell,\partial R)
>
0
$$

for nontrivial active collapse.

**Geometry-record information.**

Let \(G_\ell\) be the coarse geometry sector and let
\(R_{\partial}=(R_+,R_0,Y_{\partial})\) combine positive detections, structured
null records, and outgoing boundary data. The boundary record is syndrome-like
only if

$$
I(G_\ell;R_{\partial})>0.
$$

The dark-constraint subcase is

$$
I(G_\ell;R_0)>0
$$

whenever different geometry sectors predict different null records.

**Protected interior leakage.**

Let \(L_R\) denote protected interior microstate/logical information within a
fixed geometry sector. Early boundary records should not directly centralize
that information:

$$
I(L_R;R_{\partial}^{\mathrm{early}}\mid G_\ell)
\leq
\epsilon_L.
$$

This conditional form is stronger and cleaner than raw
\(I(L_R;R_{\partial}^{\mathrm{early}})\), because boundary records are allowed
to reveal geometry-sector information.

**Eventual decodable redistribution.**

If collapse removes coordination capacity \(\Delta C_R\) from the interior, the
outgoing boundary channel must eventually carry a recoverable record:

$$
I\!\left(
X_R;
Y_{\partial}^{[t,t+T_{\mathrm{dec}}]}
\right)
\geq
\eta\,\Delta C_R-\varepsilon.
$$

The early-time privacy condition and the late-time decodability condition are
not contradictions. They are the gravitational analog of QEC: syndrome records
can reveal error/geometry sectors before the logical/interior state is
available for authorized reconstruction.

## 8. Schur Block Reading

For any finite kernel with a boundary/interior diagnostic split, one can form a
local Gaussian or information-geometric approximation around macrocell \(m\):

$$
Q_\ell(m)
=
\begin{pmatrix}
A_\ell & B_\ell\\
B_\ell^T & D_\ell
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Here \(A_\ell\) tracks boundary-record precision, \(D_\ell\) tracks retained
interior precision, and \(B_\ell\) tracks boundary/interior coupling. The
effective boundary law is admissible only when the retained internal block is
nondegenerate on the support being eliminated:

$$
D_\ell^{-1}\ \text{exists on retained support},
\qquad
\kappa(D_\ell)<\infty.
$$

The relational kernel therefore gives the gravitational object that the Schur
bridge needs:

$$
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{rel},\ell}
\longrightarrow
\mathcal M_\ell
\longrightarrow
P_{\ell,\Delta}(m'|m)
\longrightarrow
Q_\ell/D_\ell.
$$

Singular collapse is the case where this chain breaks because the internal
block is no longer a legal object to eliminate.

## 9. Minimal Collapse Macrocell

A first semiclassical collapse implementation does not need full quantum
gravity. It needs relational bins that are less coordinate-dependent than the
toy coordinate \(z\). A minimal finite cell can use:

$$
m
=
(
M_{\partial},
J_{\partial},
Q_{\partial},
C_R,
\bar\theta_R,
\bar K_R,
A_{\partial},
N_0,
Y_{\partial}
),
$$

where:

- \(C_R=2GM_R/R_{\mathrm{areal}}\) is compactness defined using areal radius;
- \(\bar\theta_R\) is coarse expansion of an inward congruence;
- \(\bar K_R\) is a binned curvature or curvature-density diagnostic;
- \(A_{\partial}\) is a boundary or horizon-area bin;
- \(N_0\) is a structured null-record count or pattern;
- \(Y_{\partial}\) is outgoing radiation/boundary data.

The classical collapse basin is then a subset such as

$$
\mathcal B_{\mathrm{coll}}(\alpha,\beta)
=
\{m:
\bar\theta_R<-\alpha,\ C_R>\beta,\ \bar K_R\ \text{increasing}\}.
$$

`simulations/cosmic_coordination_floor/`. It replaces the original one-variable

2. hard singular-history exclusion;

## 10. Candidate-Mechanism Audit

For any proposed completion, the question is not only whether curvature is
finite. The mechanism must instantiate a kernel satisfying:

1. **Normalization:** \(P_{\ell,\Delta}\) is a genuine stochastic/CPTP
   channel on admissible relational macrocells.
2. **Floor:** active collapse obeys
   \(H_{\ell,\Delta}(m)\geq H_{\mathrm{floor}}(m)>0\).
3. **Geometry syndrome:** \(I(G_\ell;R_{\partial})>0\).
4. **Early privacy:** \(I(L_R;R_{\partial}^{\mathrm{early}}\mid G_\ell)\) is
   bounded.
5. **Late decoding:** \(I(X_R;Y_{\partial}^{[t,t+T_{\mathrm{dec}}]})\) repays
   the coordination removed from the core.
6. **Classical limit:** away from the floor, the kernel coarse-grains to the
   usual semiclassical gravitational law.

Regular black holes, loop/effective bounces, asymptotic-safety interiors,
fuzzball-like structures, islands, remnants, baby universes, and topology
change should all be scored against the same six tests.
