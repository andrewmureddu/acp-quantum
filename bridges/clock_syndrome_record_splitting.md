# Clock-Syndrome Record Splitting for Encoded Braided Clocks

*Status: formal bridge note. Propositions 1-2 and Lemma 3 are proved at the
stated level of generality (finite dimensions, exact code sector,
instrument-level). The corollary is a direct consequence. The approximate
and covariant generalizations are open and tracked as OP-30.*

## Abstract

The braided-clock program (`bridges/quantum_braiding_timekeeping.md`) asks a
single record stream to do three jobs: keep time, expose the error sector,
and leave the protected logical information alone. Experiments A-E showed
empirically that on a code these jobs separate. This note proves the
separation:

> a record channel that is transparent to the logical algebra carries
> exactly zero information about any logical clock phase; a record channel
> that carries logical clock information necessarily disturbs the conjugate
> logical coherence, with an explicit exponential tradeoff for weak binary
> probes.

Hence an encoded braided clock must split its records into two channels: a
syndrome channel, free by commutation and clock-blind by the same
commutation, and a clock channel, informative and necessarily costly. The
single monitored qubit of Experiment A is the degenerate case in which no
nontrivial transparent records exist, so one stream did both jobs badly.

## 1. Setting

Let \(\mathcal H\) be finite-dimensional, \(P\) the projector onto a code
sector \(\mathcal C = P\mathcal H\) with \(\dim\mathcal C = 2\) (one logical
qubit; the extension to \(2^k\) is verbatim). Let the logical algebra
\(\mathcal A_L\) be generated by operators \(\bar X,\bar Z\) with

$$
\bar X^2=\bar Z^2=P,
\qquad
\bar X\bar Z=-\bar Z\bar X
\quad\text{on }\mathcal C,
\qquad
\bar XP=P\bar X=\bar X,\ \ \bar ZP=P\bar Z=\bar Z .
$$

A **record channel** is a quantum instrument \(\{\mathcal I_r\}\) with Kraus
decomposition \(\mathcal I_r(\rho)=\sum_k M_{r,k}\rho M_{r,k}^\dagger\) and
outcome distribution \(p(r|\rho)=\operatorname{Tr}\mathcal I_r(\rho)\).

**Definition 1 (logically transparent instrument).** The instrument is
*logically transparent* on \(\mathcal C\) if every Kraus operator commutes
with the logical algebra on the code sector:

$$
[M_{r,k},L]\,P=0
\qquad
\text{for all } r,k \text{ and all } L\in\mathcal A_L .
$$

Weak or projective measurements of stabilizer-group elements are logically
transparent, since stabilizers commute with the logical operators; this is
the instrument-level form of the Knill-Laflamme structure used in
`bridges/otherness_preserving_recovery.md`.

**Definition 2 (logical clock).** A *logical clock* is a one-parameter
family of code states

$$
\rho_\theta=U_\theta\,\rho_0\,U_\theta^\dagger,
\qquad
U_\theta=\exp\!\left(-\tfrac{i\theta}{2}\bar G\right),
$$

with generator \(\bar G\in\mathcal A_L\), \(\bar G^2=P\), \(\bar G\) not
proportional to \(P\), and \(\rho_0=P\rho_0P\). The clock phase is
\(\theta\); a record stream \(R\) *reads the clock* if \(I(\Theta;R)>0\) for
some prior on \(\theta\), equivalently if \(p(r|\theta)\) is not constant
in \(\theta\).

## 2. Proposition 1: transparent records are clock-blind

**Proposition 1.** If the instrument is logically transparent on
\(\mathcal C\), then for every code-sector clock \(\rho_\theta\),

$$
p(r|\rho_\theta)=p(r|\rho_0)
\qquad\text{for all }\theta,
$$

and therefore \(I(\Theta;R)=0\) for every prior. The same holds for any
finite adaptive sequence of transparent instruments, including
record-conditioned (feedback) sequences, provided every applied Kraus
operator is transparent.

*Proof.* Since \(\bar G\in\mathcal A_L\), transparency gives
\(M_{r,k}U_\theta P=U_\theta M_{r,k}P\) (expand the exponential; every
power of \(\bar G\) commutes with \(M_{r,k}\) against \(P\), using
\(\bar G P=\bar G\)). With \(\rho_0=P\rho_0P\),

$$
M_{r,k}\rho_\theta M_{r,k}^\dagger
=(M_{r,k}U_\theta P)\rho_0(M_{r,k}U_\theta P)^\dagger
=U_\theta\,(M_{r,k}\rho_0M_{r,k}^\dagger)\,U_\theta^\dagger .
$$

Taking the trace and summing over \(k\) gives
\(p(r|\rho_\theta)=p(r|\rho_0)\). For a sequence, note the post-outcome
state after a transparent instrument applied to \(U_\theta\rho
U_\theta^\dagger\) is \(U_\theta\rho_r'U_\theta^\dagger\) with \(\rho_r'\)
independent of \(\theta\) (same display, before tracing), so by induction
every joint record probability \(p(r_1,\dots,r_n|\theta)\) is
\(\theta\)-independent; record-conditioned choices of later transparent
instruments inherit the property because the conditioning variables are
themselves \(\theta\)-independent. \(\square\)

**Proposition 2 (contrapositive).** If a record stream reads a logical
clock, at least one applied Kraus operator fails to commute with the clock
generator \(\bar G\) on \(\mathcal C\): the instrument is *logically
central* in the sense of `bridges/otherness_preserving_recovery.md`.

## 3. Lemma 3: the price of a clock record

Centrality is not merely qualitative; for the weak binary probes used
throughout the braided-clock simulations the tradeoff is exact.

**Lemma 3.** Let \(\bar A\in\mathcal A_L\), \(\bar A^2=P\), with code-sector
eigenprojectors \(\Pi_\pm=(P\pm\bar A)/2\), and consider the weak binary
measurement with Kraus pair

$$
M_s=\sqrt{\tfrac{1+s\kappa}{2}}\,\Pi_+
   +\sqrt{\tfrac{1-s\kappa}{2}}\,\Pi_-,
\qquad s=\pm1,\ 0<\kappa<1 .
$$

Then for any code state with \(a(\theta)=\operatorname{Tr}(\bar
A\rho_\theta)\):

1. **Record statistics.** \(p(s|\theta)=\tfrac12\,(1+s\kappa a(\theta))\),
   with Fisher information about the clock phase

   $$
   J(\theta)=\frac{\kappa^2\,a'(\theta)^2}{1-\kappa^2a(\theta)^2}
   \ \le\ \frac{\kappa^2}{1-\kappa^2}\,a'(\theta)^2 .
   $$

2. **Conjugate disturbance.** For any \(\bar B\in\mathcal A_L\) with
   \(\bar A\bar B=-\bar B\bar A\), the nonselective map
   \(\rho\mapsto\sum_sM_s\rho M_s^\dagger\) multiplies
   \(\operatorname{Tr}(\bar B\rho)\) by exactly \(\sqrt{1-\kappa^2}\).

3. **Exponential budget.** After \(N\) such probes (any adaptive schedule
   of strengths \(\kappa_i\)), total record Fisher information
   \(J_{\mathrm{tot}}\le\sum_i\kappa_i^2a'^2_{\max}/(1-\kappa_i^2)\) and
   conjugate coherence multiplier
   \(\prod_i\sqrt{1-\kappa_i^2}\le
   \exp\!\big(-\tfrac12\sum_i\kappa_i^2\big)\), hence

   $$
   C_N\ \le\
   \exp\!\left(-\,\frac{J_{\mathrm{tot}}\,(1-\kappa_{\max}^2)}
   {2\,a'^2_{\max}}\right)
   \ \xrightarrow[\ \kappa_{\max}\to0\ ]{}\
   e^{-J_{\mathrm{tot}}/2}
   \quad(\text{with }a'_{\max}\le1).
   $$

*Proof.* (1) is direct computation from \(\operatorname{Tr}(M_s\rho
M_s^\dagger)\) and the definition of Fisher information for a binary
outcome. (2): in the \(\bar A\) eigenbasis, \(\bar B\) is purely
off-diagonal; the nonselective map scales the off-diagonal block of
\(\rho\) by \(\sum_s\sqrt{\tfrac{1+s\kappa}{2}}\sqrt{\tfrac{1-s\kappa}{2}}
=\sqrt{1-\kappa^2}\). (3): additivity of Fisher information over
(conditionally independent) outcomes, multiplicativity of the disturbance
factors, \(\ln(1-\kappa^2)\le-\kappa^2\), and
\(\kappa_i^2\ge J_i(1-\kappa_i^2)/a'^2_{\max}\) from (1). For rotations
generated by \(\bar G\) conjugate to \(\bar A\), \(a(\theta)\) is a
sinusoid, so \(a'_{\max}\le1\). \(\square\)

Item (3) is the formal version of the tick-cost observation recorded
empirically in Experiments A and D: a clock statistic with total
information \(J\) (squared SNR) costs at least a factor \(e^{-J/2}\) of the
conjugate logical coherence in the gentle-probe limit. Nothing in the
argument used the physical/logical distinction, which is why the same
exponent appeared for bare-qubit ticks; the code changes *which* observables
are cheap, not the price of centrality.

## 4. Corollary: record splitting

**Corollary 4 (record splitting).** Let an encoded braided clock be
required to satisfy the braided persistence criterion of
`bridges/quantum_braiding_timekeeping.md` §7 over an operating window:
(i) nonzero clock information in its records, (ii) logical memory
retention above a floor \(F_{\mathrm{floor}}\), and (iii) syndrome
information with near-zero conditional logical leakage. Then:

1. No single logically transparent record stream satisfies (i)
   (Proposition 1).
2. No single logically central stream of weak binary probes satisfies (ii)
   and (iii) once its total clock information exceeds
   \(J_{\max}=-2\ln F_{\mathrm{floor}}\) in the gentle limit (Lemma 3.3),
   and a central stream is by definition not leakage-free with respect to
   the logical algebra it reads.
3. Therefore the record stream must split into at least two channels: a
   transparent syndrome channel — clock-blind and cost-free by the same
   commutation — and a central clock channel whose information is bounded
   by the memory budget, \(J_{\mathrm{clock}}\le-2\ln F_{\mathrm{floor}}\)
   per protected coherence lifetime.

The split is not an engineering choice; it is forced by the commutant
structure. In ACP terms this is a two-channel instance of the A.20
restraint-power floor: the coordination that keeps time is bought with
exactly the resource — conjugate logical coherence — that the syndrome
channel is designed to protect, so a persistent encoded clock must
allocate its record budget across a protected and an unprotected register
rather than optimize a single stream.

**Degenerate case.** For the trivial code \(P=I\) on one qubit,
\(\mathcal A_L\) is the full operator algebra and the only transparent
instruments are trivial (Kraus operators proportional to unitaries in the
center, i.e., no information at all). Every informative record is central.
This is Experiment A: the single monitored qubit had no transparent
channel available, so its one stream necessarily paid coherence for every
bit of both clock and syndrome information — the origin of its narrow,
partial-lock productive interval.

## 5. Relation to known results

⚠ Honesty note. The tension between exact codes and covariant clock
information is not new: Eastin-Knill-type obstructions, covariant-QEC
no-go theorems, and clock/code tradeoff results (e.g. Faist et al.,
Woods-Alhambra) establish related and in some directions much stronger
statements for continuous symmetries and reference frames. Propositions
1-2 are best read as a finite, instrument-level, ACP-packaged cousin of
that structure, with two local contributions: the explicit
Fisher-information/coherence budget of Lemma 3 in the exact form used by
the braided-clock simulations, and the record-splitting reading — clock
and syndrome as complementary record channels inside one persistence
criterion. No claim of priority is made for the underlying commutation
argument.

## 6. Numerical anchors

The propositions are already instantiated by the seeded simulations in
`simulations/quantum_braiding_clock/`:

- Proposition 1: in Experiment E, 72 weak stabilizer measurements leave
  logical-\(\bar Y\) retention at `0.9967` (unit up to decoder false
  fires), independent of everything logical.
- Proposition 2 / Lemma 3: the `bare_monitored` policy — the same probe
  strength applied to an anticommuting observable — retains `0.0008`,
  matching \((1-\kappa^2)^{N/2}=(1-0.36)^{18}\approx3.3\times10^{-4}\) at
  the resolution of 600 trajectories.
- Lemma 3.3: the Experiment B budget optimum \(B\approx1.05\) is the
  empirical solution of the tradeoff the lemma bounds: spend
  \(J_{\mathrm{clock}}\) up to, not past, the memory floor.

## 7. Open problems

**OP-30 (approximate and covariant record splitting).** Extend
Propositions 1-2 beyond the exact code sector: (a) approximate QEC —
bound \(I(\Theta;R)\) for instruments that are \(\epsilon\)-transparent
(commutators small in norm) on an \(\epsilon\)-approximate code, ideally
with a continuity bound \(I(\Theta;R)\le f(\epsilon)\); (b) covariant
codes — reconcile the record-splitting corollary with covariant-QEC
tradeoffs when the clock generator is a symmetry the code partially
respects; (c) the operational-time link (OP-29) — the clock channel
defines the system's tempo \(\nu_S\), the syndrome channel cannot, so the
proper productive interval of an encoded clock is measured by its central
channel alone; state this as a covariance-compatible definition.
